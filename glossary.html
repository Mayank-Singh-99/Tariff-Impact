The vocabulary, in plain language

Every abbreviation on this site, defined once. Search it, or filter by category. Where a term is routinely confused with a similar one, the difference is stated, because most of the disagreement in this story turns on exactly that.

Why so many terms

US trade law separates national-security measures from country-specific ones, and each has its own statute, its own procedure and its own rate. The distinctions are legally meaningful and frequently blurred in reporting, which is where a lot of the confusion about “the Indian tariff rate” comes from.

The one distinction that matters most

Every rate on this site is additional to the standard MFN duty unless the text says otherwise. A 50% additional duty is not a 50% total duty, and the landed cost of an Indian good facing a 50% penalty is the MFN rate, the additional rate, and the compounding effect of the two.
