Important caveat

What did not change

The episode above concerns country-specific duties. A separate layer of US tariffs applied throughout on national-security grounds, to all trading partners including India, and was not altered at any point in this period.

Section 232 duties, unchanged
Apply to all US trading partners, not India specifically.

Why this distinction matters

A headline figure of “50%” in August 2025 refers to the country-specific additional duty. It does not include Section 232, which applies separately by product. Conversely, the 100% rate on patented pharmaceuticals never touched Indian generics, which is why the pharma sector reads as insulated throughout.
